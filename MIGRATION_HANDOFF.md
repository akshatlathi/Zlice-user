## 1. THE NORTH STAR: "CAMPUS OS"
**Zlice is NOT a food delivery app.**
If you treat it like one, you have failed.
**Zlice is the Operating System for Residential Campuses.**

- **The Problem:** 22,000 students, 8.7km², isolated community. Swiggy fails here (unit economics don't work).
- **The Solution:** **Batch Economics.**
    - Swiggy: 1 order/trip = ₹12 margin (Loss).
    - Zlice: 15 orders/trip (Batched at 11:30 PM) = ₹180 margin (Profit).
- **The End Game:** Food is just the hook ("Milk in the grocery store"). We capture the user with night food, then sell them Transport, Community, and Governance.

## 2. THE ADVERSARIAL DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
**Target User:** IIT Students (Top 0.1% IQ).
**Behavior:** They are optimization machines. They *will* gamify, exploit, and reverse-engineer any system we build.

**Security by Design:**
- **The Multi-Layer Defense:**
    - **Shadowbanning:** Never hard-ban exploiters. Just silently stop their points from accruing. Let them scream into the void.
    - **Velocity Limits:** 3 orders/day. 15-min cooldowns. Prevents "dorm farming" (pooling orders).
    - **Emulator Detection:** Check battery usage and gyroscope. Real phones move; emulators are static.

## 3. BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS (The "Addiction" Layer)
We don't sell food; we sell **Dopamine**.

### **A. Endowed Progress Effect**
- **Rule:** Never show a user "0 Points".
- **Implementation:** Sign-up bonus gives them 50% of the first reward instantly. "You are 50% of the way to Free Chai!" (Conversion jumps 2x).

### **B. Variable Ratio Reinforcement (The "Slot Machine")**
- **Mechanism:** "Aura Spin" after every order.
- **Why:** Predictable rewards are boring. Unpredictable rewards (5 Aura? 100 Aura?) create habit loops.
- **Math:** 60% chance of small reward, 3% chance of "Jackpot".

### **C. Loss Aversion (Streak Economy)**
- **Mechanism:** Streak Freeze.
- **Psychology:** Students will pay distinct currency (Aura) to save a 50-day streak. It's an ego asset.
- **Inflation Control:** As Aura supply increases, the price of Streak Freezes rises. It's our "Interest Rate".

---

## 4. THE AURA ECONOMY (Business Model)
**Aura is a Partner-Funded Currency.**
- **Minting:** Users earn Aura when they order (Funded by our margin).
- **Burning:** Users redeem Aura for coupons (Funded by Partners).
    - *Example:* Cafe Coffee Day gives 100 "Free Coffee" coupons. Cost to Zlice = ₹0. Perceived Value >₹100.
- **Zlice Revenue:** We take **100%** of the revenue from redemptions that we sell (if any) or 100% of the data value.
